This app is designed to provide the new Grade 8 Citizenship Education book in an easy-to-use format that anyone can access on their phone.

The purpose of organizing this app is to ensure that everyone, whether at home or abroad, can easily access and use it.

The main topics covered in this app include:

  • Civic Virtues
  • State and Government Administration
  • Democracy
  • Constitutionalism
  • Human Rights
  • Conflict Resolution and Peacebuilding
  • Critical Thinking and Problem Solving Skills
